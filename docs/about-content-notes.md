# About EGAILE content and imagery

Updated 17 October 2026.

## Editorial decisions

The primary audience is small and medium organisations in Delhi NCR, including service businesses, local enterprises and software teams. NCR locations describe the intended service area, not physical office locations.

The supplied screenshots were references for a four-stage interaction and clear review checkpoints. The content has been rewritten for SMEs: understand and align, design a practical plan, pilot and put it to work, review and improve. Fixed week counts, guaranteed ROI, enterprise review boards, co-located engineering pods and zero-downtime promises were omitted because EGAILE has not established those commitments.

Nasscom's SME research informed the emphasis on practical use cases, manageable adoption and appropriate investment. The Ministry of MSME's TEAM material provides context on smaller enterprises using connected digital services. These sources are background research, not evidence of EGAILE results or an endorsement of EGAILE. No numerical market claims were added.

## Generated assets

`public/images/egaile-sme-workshop.webp`: illustrative Indian SME owner and consultants mapping a process.

`public/images/egaile-sme-operations.webp`: illustrative small-workshop colleagues reviewing a job sheet and tablet.

Both assets were created with the image-generation tool for this project, then resized and encoded as WebP for delivery. They depict generic workplaces, not actual EGAILE staff, offices or clients. The public caption identifies them as AI-generated illustrations. Neither uses an external stock-photo licence or a client testimonial.

## Editing and persistence

The About section is an editable homepage section. The dedicated About page reuses it. The four-stage method is shared by the homepage, About and Our approach pages. The admin editor supports headings, body, items, photo URLs, image descriptions, disclosure caption, stage takeaways and review checkpoints. Upload buttons use the existing media endpoint and require configured cloud Storage; asset URLs work on the current build.

The requested content was applied separately to saved draft and public snapshots so unrelated draft changes were not published. Recovery revisions were saved, with an audit event. The resulting site version is 4.
